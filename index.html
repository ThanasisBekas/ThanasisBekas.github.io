Thoughts

Why start from nothing?

What kind of team do you want?

What if it isn't your stack?

I don't treat the stack as my identity, though. When a problem needs something else, I learn it.

Why banking work?

I like that kind of work: nobody notices when it's right, and everybody does when it's wrong.

Do you trust the machines?

I review every agent-written diff myself before it goes into a pull request, use tests as the gate, and keep agents out of security-, money- and regulation-sensitive code.
